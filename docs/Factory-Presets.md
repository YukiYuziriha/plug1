# HEAVEN factory presets

The browser groups 30 patches into Originals, Lead-ready, and Wild. The ten
originals keep their names and order, but have stronger signatures: more stack
in Deep Double, more crushed edge in Grit Adlibs, longer audible repeats in
Night Echo, and more distinct spaces in Shimmer Bloom, Synthetic Church and
Warm Plate. Clean Vocal Utility stays centered and emphasizes dynamics rather
than doubling. Loading a revised factory patch uses the new settings;
previously saved sessions and user presets retain their stored settings.

Try the Wild bank on an ad-lib or an automated insert. Use Global Mix to blend
back the original without changing the relationships inside a patch. Sync patches
follow host tempo. Granular patches need live input to fill their buffers; gate
and distortion intensity depend on how hard the vocal hits them.

## Production references

- [Rob Kinelski on mixing Billie Eilish](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-billie-eilish-bad-guy): separate lead, doubles and harmony groups; stereo width without a long reverb; and blending clean and distorted vocals. These are the ideas behind the tighter lead patches and parallel grit.
- [Soundtoys MicroShift](https://www.soundtoys.com/product/microshift/): pitch and short, time-varying delays for width. A technique reference for the tight stacks, not a claim of sound matching.
- [Ableton Live Audio Effect Reference](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-audio-effect-reference/): ducked echo, grains, bit reduction, pitched feedback reverb, and gated tails. These were starting points for contrasting the rhythm and texture of the Wild patches.

These are original settings, not reproductions of artists' session chains.
